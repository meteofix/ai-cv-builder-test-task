# CV template — one HTML for the preview and the PDF

**Area:** `packages/cv-template/src/**` and `packages/cv-template/test/**`, except the main-only files `src/index.ts`, `src/pdf-options.ts`, `src/node.ts` and `src/fonts.ts`. **Frozen at S0:** the signatures of `renderCvHtml(document, { fonts })`, `renderCvBody(document)`, `renderCvShell({ fonts }, lang?, title?)`, `PDF_OPTIONS`, `FontSource` and `loadEmbeddedFonts()`. **Reference:** `docs/design/cv-template/resume-a-en.html` and `resume-b-uk.html`. Decisions: [015](../DECISIONS.md#015-the-cv-keeps-the-source-language), [019](../DECISIONS.md#019-one-html-template-for-preview-and-pdf), [020](../DECISIONS.md#020-embedded-fonts-loaded-before-printing), 036 (lists are groups).

## Goal

The CV looks like the reference on screen and in the PDF, in English and Ukrainian, and stays readable for applicant tracking systems: section titles extract as whole words, sections keep their order, dates stay next to their positions.

## Contracts

- `renderCvBody(document)` — the inner HTML of `<body>`: what the live preview writes into its iframe on every edit.
- `renderCvShell({ fonts }, lang = 'en', title = '')` — a complete page without content (`<html lang>`, `<title>`, `@font-face` rules and the template CSS); the preview sets it once.
- `renderCvHtml(document, { fonts })` — `renderCvShell` with `lang = document.locale` and `title` = `"<fullName> — <headline>"` (empty parts and the dash left out), with the body inserted: what the PDF endpoint prints.
- `PDF_OPTIONS = { format: 'A4', printBackground: true, tagged: true }` — used by the PDF endpoint and by the ATS test here. The page margin (17 mm) and size come from the template's `@page` rule.
- Fonts: IBM Plex Sans 400/500/600, Latin and Cyrillic (`fonts/*.woff2`, OFL). `{ baseUrl: '/fonts' }` in the preview, `{ embedded }` (base64) in the PDF.
- `PAGE_MARGIN_MM = 17` and `PAGE_CONTENT_HEIGHT_PX` (the printable height of one PDF page in CSS px, (297 − 2 × 17) mm ≈ 994 px) in `src/page.ts`: the `@page` margin and the preview's padding come from the first; the editor divides the preview's content height by the second to show the page count. A Chromium test checks the second against a real PDF. Not yet re-exported from `src/index.ts` (a main-only file).

## Behaviour

1. **Markup and CSS** follow the reference as they are: class names, element types, the order of elements and the CSS of `resume-a-en.html` go into `src/render.tsx` and `src/styles.ts`. Visual changes are made in the reference first, never only in code. No element with text has a `position` other than `static` (nor a `transform`, `opacity` or `float` that gives it its own paint layer): Chromium paints such boxes after the normal flow, so their text would come last in the PDF and an applicant tracking system would read every bullet after the last section. The bullet dot is an inline box with a negative margin. The guard is a regression test (`test/ats-reading-order.test.ts`), not a lint rule.
2. **Empty means no text other than spaces**: a blank string, a blank bullet or line, a group with no line left (its title too), an entry with nothing to show and a section with nothing left are not rendered, and a section whose title is blank has no heading. **Sections**, in this order, each left out when empty: header, summary, experience, education, skills, then one `cv-section--extra` per `additional` section in document order. Section titles come from `LABELS[locale]`: en `Summary`, `Experience`, `Education`, `Skills`; uk `Про мене`, `Досвід роботи`, `Освіта`, `Навички`. `additional` titles are the document's own.
3. **Header.** `h1.cv-name` = full name; `p.cv-headline` only when the headline is not empty; `ul.cv-contacts` with, in this order and only when not empty: email as `<a href="mailto:…">address</a>`, phone as `<a href="tel:…">` (the `href` keeps only `+` and digits, the text stays as written), location as text, each link as `<a href="…">` whose text is the link without the scheme, `www.` stays, and without a trailing slash (`https://github.com/x/` → `github.com/x`). No empty `<li>`; no `ul` when there is nothing to list.
4. **Dates.** `YYYY` prints as is; `YYYY-MM` prints the month abbreviation and the year: en `Jan Feb Mar Apr May Jun Jul Aug Sep Oct Nov Dec`; uk (CLDR, without dots) `січ лют бер квіт трав черв лип серп вер жовт лист груд`. A range is `start – end` with a spaced en dash; `current` replaces the end with `Present` / `дотепер`. Only one side known → that side alone. Nothing known → no `.cv-entry__dates` element.
5. **Experience entry** (`article.cv-entry`): `h3.cv-entry__title` = title; the dates span; `p.cv-entry__meta` with `span.cv-entry__org` = company and `span.cv-entry__location` only when there is a location (no `p` when both are empty); then the position's groups of bullets (decision 036), in order: a group with a title prints `h4.cv-entry__group-title` right before its `ul.cv-entry__bullets`, a group without a title is the `ul.cv-entry__bullets` alone, so one untitled group looks as the reference. No `ul` when there are no bullets. The text order is title, dates, company, then per group its title and its bullets.
   5a. **Positions at one company** (owner's decision R0-GROUP, 2026-10-29; the document schema is unchanged): consecutive positions whose company is the same (ignoring case and spacing, `experienceGroups` in `@cv/shared`) render as one `article.cv-entry.cv-entry--group`: first `p.cv-entry__meta` with `span.cv-entry__org` = the company once (and `span.cv-entry__location` when every position gives the same non-empty location), then the company's period `span.cv-entry__dates.cv-entry__dates--company` on the right: the earliest start of its positions to the latest end, or to `Present` / `дотепер` when any position is current, formatted as in 4 (no span when no position has a date); then per position a `div.cv-entry__role` with its `div.cv-entry__head` (title and dates; a location of its own when the positions give different ones) and its own groups of bullets as in 5. When only the block's first position has bullets (`sharesBullets` in `@cv/shared`) they are the company's shared bullets: its groups follow the last role. A role with neither title nor dates has no head. The text order in the PDF is company, period, titles with dates, bullets. A single position renders as in 5.
6. **Education entry** (`article.cv-entry.cv-entry--compact`): title = degree, org = institution; without a degree the institution is the title and there is no meta line.
7. **Skills** come in groups (decision 035): one group without a title prints as `ul.cv-skills`, one `li` per skill; otherwise each group is a `div.cv-skill-group` with `span.cv-skill-group__title` (`Title:`, only when the title is not empty) and its `ul.cv-skills`, in document order, so the text reads `Cloud: AWS · GCP`. A titled group's skills start on the line of its title and wrap under it, each skill whole (a title on a line of its own wastes a line per group); only a first skill wider than the rest of the title's line starts below it. A title saved with its own trailing colon gets no second one, and a title of colons only is no title. A skill without text is left out, and so is a group with none left. **Additional** sections hold entries (a project, a course, a certificate), in order. An entry with a title or dates is an `article.cv-entry` like a position: `div.cv-entry__head` with `h3.cv-entry__title` and the dates span (start – end as in 4; an entry is never current), then its groups as in 5 (`h4.cv-entry__group-title` + `ul.cv-entry__bullets`); an entry without lines (a course) is `article.cv-entry.cv-entry--compact` with its head only. An entry with lines but no title and no dates (what a flat list of the earlier shape became) prints its groups as the reference's `ul.cv-list`, one `li` per line, with an `h4.cv-entry__group-title` before a titled group.
8. **Safety.** All text is escaped (React); links use only the schemes the document schema allows; no `<script>`, no inline event handlers, no external URLs besides `baseUrl` fonts. React keys are item ids, so duplicate texts do not collide.
9. **Preview = PDF.** The same CSS serves both; the preview shows one continuous A4-wide page, only the PDF paginates.
10. **Summary paragraphs and page breaks** (additions the reference has no analogue for). The summary may hold `\n` between paragraphs; `p.cv-summary` has `white-space: pre-line`, so they show as line breaks. Page breaks: an entry may break between its lines — a company block or a long position that does not fit the rest of a page starts on that page instead of leaving it nearly empty; an education entry and an entry without lines (`cv-entry--compact`) stay whole. A heading never ends a page: the section title, `.cv-entry__head`, `.cv-entry__meta` (the company line) and `.cv-entry__group-title` have `break-after: avoid`, and every `li` has `break-inside: avoid`, so a bullet never splits across pages. The guard is a Chromium test (`test/page-breaks.test.ts`).

## Acceptance examples

Test files in `packages/cv-template/test/` (`template.acceptance.test.ts` and `ats.acceptance.test.ts`; the ATS test uses real Chromium and `unpdf`). Test names start with the example id. "The en sample" is a `CvDocument` with the content of `resume-a-en.html`, "the uk sample" one with the content of `resume-b-uk.html`; both live in `test/fixtures/`.

- **TPL-1 — the en reference.** `renderCvBody(en sample)` has the sections with classes `cv-section--summary`, `--experience`, `--education`, `--skills` and three `--extra` in this order; their `h2` texts are `Summary`, `Experience`, `Education`, `Skills`, `Certifications`, `Languages`, `Volunteering`; the first position's dates read `Mar 2023 – Present`, the second's `Jan 2021 – Feb 2023`; the first education entry's `2017 – 2019`.
- **TPL-2 — the uk reference.** `renderCvHtml(uk sample, { fonts: { baseUrl: '/fonts' } })` starts with `<!DOCTYPE html><html lang="uk">`, has `<title>Олег Савчук — Frontend-розробник</title>`, section titles `Про мене`, `Досвід роботи`, `Освіта`, `Навички`, and the first position's dates `вер 2022 – дотепер`.
- **TPL-3 — empty elements are not rendered.** A document with an empty headline, an empty phone and location, no links, a position without dates, location and bullets, no skills and an empty summary → the HTML has no `cv-headline`, no empty `<li>`, no `cv-entry__dates`, no `cv-entry__location`, no `cv-entry__bullets`, no `cv-section--skills`, no `cv-section--summary`, and no `–`.
- **TPL-4 — dates.**
  | locale | start     | end       | current | printed                |
  | ------ | --------- | --------- | ------- | ---------------------- |
  | en     | `2020-05` | null      | true    | `May 2020 – Present`   |
  | en     | `2019`    | `2021-02` | false   | `2019 – Feb 2021`      |
  | en     | `2018-07` | null      | false   | `Jul 2018`             |
  | uk     | `2023-11` | `2024-01` | false   | `лист 2023 – січ 2024` |
  | uk     | `2022-09` | null      | true    | `вер 2022 – дотепер`   |
- **TPL-5 — contacts and links.** Email `iryna@example.com`, phone `+1 (555) 010-0142`, location `Kyiv, Ukraine`, links `https://github.com/ikovalenko/` and `https://www.linkedin.com/in/iryna` → `<a href="mailto:iryna@example.com">iryna@example.com</a>`, `<a href="tel:+15550100142">+1 (555) 010-0142</a>`, `Kyiv, Ukraine`, `<a href="https://github.com/ikovalenko/">github.com/ikovalenko</a>`, `<a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/iryna">www.linkedin.com/in/iryna</a>`, in this order.
- **TPL-6 — ATS readability, en and uk.** Each sample → `renderCvHtml` with embedded fonts → Chromium `page.pdf(PDF_OPTIONS)` → text with `unpdf`: every section title appears as a whole word (`Experience`, not `E x p e r i e n c e`; `Досвід роботи` as written); the titles appear in document order; for every position, its printed dates appear after its title and before the next position's title.
- **TPL-7 — text is text.** A bullet `<script>alert(1)</script>` renders escaped; the HTML contains no `<script`.
- **TPL-8 — one page for both.** `renderCvHtml(doc, opts)` contains `<body>` + `renderCvBody(doc)` + `</body>` and exactly one `<head>`, also when a bullet reads ``Saved $$ on hosting: a $& b $` c $' d`` (a string replacement would expand those sequences; build the expected value by concatenation, not with a replace call).

- **TPL-9 — a company with several positions.** Positions `Lead Engineer` (Jan 2022 – Jun 2024, one bullet) and `Engineer` (Mar 2019 – Dec 2021, no bullets) at `Acme Corp`, then `Developer` at `Globex` → one `cv-entry--group` with `Acme Corp` once, the two titles with their dates, then the bullet after the last title; `Globex` renders as an ordinary entry. When both positions have bullets, each follows its own title. In the PDF of the en sample (which ends with two positions at one company), the company comes before both titles and their dates, and the shared bullet after them.
- **TPL-10 — skill groups.** The en sample (groups Languages, Frontend, Backend, Tooling) renders four `div.cv-skill-group`, each with its title followed by a colon and its skills in order; the uk sample (one group without a title) renders one `ul.cv-skills` and no `cv-skill-group`. In the PDF text of the en sample (as TPL-6) the groups follow each other in order, each title before its own skills.

The existing `test/pdf-options.test.ts` (A4 size, 17 mm margin, the frozen `PDF_OPTIONS`) stays as it is.

## Definition of done

The acceptance examples pass; the rendered en and uk samples look like the reference files side by side in a browser at A4 width and at 360 px inside the preview; the PDF endpoint (pdf.md) prints the same HTML; this spec matches the code.

## Required tests

The acceptance examples; a duplicate bullet text in one position renders twice without a React key warning; a titled group prints its title before its bullets; an extra entry prints its title, dates and lines; a company block prints its period (earliest – latest, `Present` when current); summary line breaks survive with `pre-line`; in the PDF a company block that does not fit the rest of page 1 starts on page 1 and no page ends with a heading or half a bullet; `PAGE_CONTENT_HEIGHT_PX` matches the printed page.
